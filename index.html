Welcome to the website of Chilworth Village Hall, near Guildford in the Surrey Hills.

On this website you will find a host of information about our hall.

Discover more

About Chilworth Village Hall was given to the community in 1922. Located in the heart of the village, the hall is easily accessible and offers a car park, a spacious hall with vaulted ceiling, a fully fitted kitchen, a raised stage, and tables and chairs for your use. It is a registered charity, with a board of trustees, and a volunteer management committee that looks after the day-to-day running of the hall.

Facilities

The Village Hall is a versatile venue suitable for a wide range of activities and events. It also offers wheelchair and pushchair access throughout. Please click on the images below to explore the venue in more detail.

Hall

The main hall is a spacious venue featuring a raised stage with curtains and lighting, a surround sound system, a piano, and two small side rooms. Tables and chairs are also available for use.

Kitchen

The kitchen is available for warming food and is equipped with a cooker, microwave, hot drinks dispenser, and food and drink storage. Crockery and cutlery are provided free of charge.

Parking/Grounds

The hall has a hard standing car park which can also be closed off for use as an outside area.

How to Find Us

Our Location

Chilworth Village Hall, New Road, Chilworth, Guildford, Surrey, GU4 8LZ

Directions from Guildford

The Village Hall is approximately half a mile from the entrance to the village and can be found on the right-hand side of the road. A bus shelter is situated directly outside the building.

From Dorking

Follow the A25 through Albury Village and continue into Chilworth along Dorking Road. The Village Hall is located approximately 200 metres beyond the level crossing on the left-hand side of the road.

Bookings & Enquiries

The cost of hiring the hall is £10 per hour Monday to Friday and £11 per hour at weekends.

Please refer to the calendar when making a booking. We have slots reserved for our regular users. However, some bookings only apply during term time, and availability changes regularly. If you cannot find a suitable slot, please get in touch — we will be happy to help if we can.
